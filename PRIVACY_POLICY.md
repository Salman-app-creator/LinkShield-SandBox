# Privacy Policy — LinkShield Sandbox

**Last Updated:** 10 September 2026

## Introduction

LinkShield Sandbox ("we", "our", "the app") is a privacy-first browser that opens links in an isolated sandbox environment. This policy explains how we handle your data.

## Data We Do NOT Collect

- ❌ Browsing history
- ❌ Passwords or form data
- ❌ Cookies or sessions
- ❌ Personal information
- ❌ Location data
- ❌ Contact lists
- ❌ Device identifiers for tracking

## Data We DO Process

### 1. URL Threat Checking
When you open a URL, we send it to:
- Google Safe Browsing API
- URLhaus (abuse.ch)
- AlienVault OTX
- ThreatFox (abuse.ch)
- PhishStats

These services check if the URL is malicious. We do not store these URLs.

### 2. Media Downloads (Grabber)
When you download media, the URL is sent to our self-hosted server (yt-dlp/Cobalt). The media file is delivered directly to your device's Downloads folder.

### 3. License Validation (Pro Users)
If you activate Pro, your license key is validated against our GitHub-hosted key list.

## Data Storage

- All browsing data is deleted when you close the app
- No analytics or tracking SDKs
- No advertising networks
- No third-party data sharing

## Permissions

- **INTERNET**: Required for browsing and threat checking
- **POST_NOTIFICATIONS**: For download progress notifications
- **WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE**: For saving media files (Android 9 and below)

## Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
