## What is LinkShield?

LinkShield Sandbox is a **privacy-first browser** that opens every link you click in an **isolated, secure environment** — like a quarantine room for websites.

Your real browser (Chrome) stays untouched. No passwords auto-fill. No cookies saved. No tracking. Just safe browsing.

---

## Why Use It?

| Without LinkShield | With LinkShield |
|---|---|
| Fake login pages steal your passwords | Auto-fill blocked — scammers get nothing |
| Websites track you with cookies | Cookies deleted when you close |
| Malware can infect your phone | Isolated environment — virus can't escape |
| Browsing history saved forever | No history left behind |

## Features

- **Isolated WebView** — Every link opens in a separate sandbox
- **Phishing Protection** — Fake sites can't access your saved passwords
- **No Data Persistence** — Close the app, all session data is gone
- **HTTPS Security Indicator** — Green lock shows secure connections
- **Built-in Navigation** — Back, forward, refresh, and share
- **Error Handling** — Clear messages for no internet or broken links
- **Lightweight** — Under 7 MB

## How to Install

1. **Download** the APK from [Releases](../../releases)
2. **Open** the file — tap "Install" (allow "Unknown sources" if asked)
3. **Open LinkShield** → Tap **"Enable Link Protection"**
4. **Set as Default Browser** when prompted
5. **Done!** Now every link opens safely
